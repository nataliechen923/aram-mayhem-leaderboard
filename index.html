How the ranking is fair — read this before you complain

Worked example — Nov 8 snapshot

Ohwutahel (tanks) vs Vincent Chan (all carry champs). Vincent ranks 2nd at 55.4 and Ohwutahel 4th at 47.9 — and that gap is not because carries get more kills. Vincent is graded only against other Damage games, Ohwutahel's tank games only against other Frontline games.

Ohwutahel's own numbers prove the point: his Frontline games grade 52.4 (+4.4, 52 games), while his Damage games grade 44.3 and his Support games 41.9. As a frontliner he is actually close to Vincent's level — his overall rank is pulled down by the off-role games in his last 100, not by playing tanks. If he played Frontline only, his score would be around 52, not 47.9.

Vincent's rank comes from carry games graded within the carry role: his Senna grades 70.1 (+13.3, 5 games), and his loss-vs-close gap is the smallest in the group (51.1 vs 56.0), which is what the median + floor terms reward. A carry with big KDA but a low floor would not rank this high — KDA is only 1 of 7 criteria, and win rate is only 15% of the score.

Player by Player — what they do well, and what to work on

Each card shows why the player stands where they do, then one “What to work on” block — computed only from that player's most recent 20 games, compared with the group's recent-20 averages, and split into games played with 2+ of the group versus solo games, so stacked and solo play don't get mixed up. Top 3 / Worst 3 champions — last 50 games

One card per player: Best on the left, Worst on the right, three round portraits each. Ranked by mean game grade on that champion versus the player's own average. Only champions with 3+ games count, so a one-game fluke can't top the list. If a player has only a few champions with 3+ games, the same champion can appear on both sides — it just means that champion is both the best and the worst of a small set. Grade, record and delta are the hard facts under each portrait; the small line under them says why it lands there.

How the Rating Works

Where this data comes from: the numbers on this page right now are a Nov 8, 2026 snapshot copied from the original page (bboyisk.github.io/aram-mayhem-assessment), which sourced its per-player history from PlayARAM and its population baselines from MayhemMeta. This page never contacts PlayARAM — nothing here was scraped from it after it blocked us. Future updates come from each player's PlayARAM profile, fetched once a day by GitHub Actions (no Riot API key needed — Riot's API does not reliably serve ARAM Mayhem), with a manual CSV export as backup. PlayARAM's match list shows result, champion, KDA, CS and skillshots dodged, but not damage or turret takedowns, so Output and Conversion stay blank on PlayARAM games — a missing field is skipped, never scored as 0. Subscores, recent-20, top/worst champions and per-game rows stay blank until that first update — no values were invented to fill them.

Data: per-player history from PlayARAM (playaram.gg), fetched daily. Population champion & augment win rates: MayhemMeta (mayhemmeta.com). Skillshots dodged comes from PlayARAM's match list.

What isn't measured: PlayARAM does not publish healing, shielding, crowd-control score or per-player objective damage, so none of those appear here.
